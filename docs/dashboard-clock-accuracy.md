# Dashboard clock accuracy

Saved lifecycle dates belong to their recorded stage. Missing dates are unknown;
folder modification times and dates from another stage are not substituted.
The Review page reports missing dates, invalid dates, unmatched tracking records
and case-only candidates. It does not alter clocks or remove stale records.

A successful scan can discover new titles and reset clocks after verified content
changes. Future-dated TV content blocks the affected show, preserving its clock.
Read-only observations cannot establish that external writers were excluded.
Tracking records without folders may reflect external moves, renames or deletions;
resolve them explicitly rather than automatically discarding their history.

[Tracking Review](tracking-review.md) provides explicit archival of a stale clock
after checking all four stage folders. The original date remains in archived
tracking history. A returning folder gets a new active clock.
