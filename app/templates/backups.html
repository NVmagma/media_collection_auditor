Verification checks the selected snapshot, not current-state freshness. Test restore copy writes a new private directory under .auditor-backups/.restore-tests and compares bytes. It does not start a worker, test live recovery, replace current files, clear pending operations, or contact media services.

No automatic retention or deletion. Up to 100 backup directories and 100 test copies are retained; reaching the limit requires manual review. There is no active restore or purge control on this page.
